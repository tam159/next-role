How it works

Six stages. One conversation.

The career agent runs the pipeline and delegates the specialist work. Tailoring and coaching run in parallel, and follow-up edits go straight to the agent that owns the file.

Edits don't rerun the pipeline. "Add a fourth round" goes to the interview coach; "add React to my skills" goes to the resume tailor. Each one reads the existing file, preserves everything you didn't name, and re-renders.

Meet the agents

One coordinator, three specialists.

Each specialist is declared in a YAML file with its own model, tools, and skills, and only receives the tools it opts into.

Career agent coordinator Runs intake, parses your documents, delegates, and assembles the battlecard.
hiring-recon research Live web research on the company and the role, distilled into a match analysis.
resume-tailor writing Rewrites your CV against the JD and research, then renders the PDF.
interview-coach coaching Writes the self-introduction and STAR stories for every round.

A second analytics agent in the agent switcher answers questions about the product itself with read-only SQL and charts.

Open source

Self-hosted. Yours to run today.

NextRole is MIT-licensed and runs end to end on your own machine while the hosted version is being built. Your CV and your data never leave your infrastructure.

  • Bring your own model. Swap providers at runtime, or go fully local with Ollama or LM Studio.
  • Human approval for shell commands. Risky commands pause for approve, edit, or reject.
  • Multi-user mode, opt-in. Google or email login with per-user threads, files, and memory.
  • Reachable by other agents. The career agent is exposed over MCP and A2A, no extra code.
  • Built on the LangChain stack. LangGraph, DeepAgents, and LangSmith tracing out of the box.
