Privacy Policy

Effective 1 November 2026

NextRole ("we", "us") is a career assistant built and run by an independent developer. This policy covers the hosted app at app.next-role.dev and this website. If you run the open-source code on your own server, you are the operator and this policy does not apply to your copy.

Questions or requests: hello@next-role.dev.

What we collect

  • Your Google account basics. When you sign in with Google we receive your name, email address, profile picture, and Google account ID. We do not request access to your Gmail, Drive, Calendar, or contacts.
  • What you give the assistant. CVs, job descriptions, and other files you upload; your messages; and everything the assistant produces for you (research notes, tailored resumes, interview prep, battlecards, PDFs).
  • Saved preferences. Short notes the assistant keeps about how you like your work done, so later conversations follow them. You can ask it to change or remove them.
  • Usage records. For each request: time, model, token counts, and estimated cost. We use them to enforce fair-use limits.
  • Sign-in sessions. A session cookie, plus the IP address and browser user agent recorded with each session, for security.

How we use it

Only to run the service for you: answer your requests, store your files and conversations so you can come back to them, enforce usage limits, and keep the service secure. We do not sell your data, show ads, or use your content to train models.

Who processes it

These providers process data on our behalf, each under its own privacy terms. We share only what each one needs to do its job.

  • Vultr: hosts the app servers and database (Singapore).
  • Cloudflare: DNS, encryption in transit, and protection for all traffic; stores your uploaded and generated files (private storage, never public); hosts this website.
  • Google: sign-in.
  • Anthropic: the AI model (Claude). Your messages, the contents of your files, and the assistant's working notes are sent to it to produce answers.
  • LlamaIndex (LlamaCloud): converts uploaded documents (for example a PDF CV) into text.
  • Tavily or TinyFish: web search. Search queries, such as a company name or role title, are sent; your files are not.

These providers are located in several countries, including the United States and Singapore, so your data may be processed outside the country where you live.

Google user data

We use the information we receive from Google only to create your account, sign you in, and show your name and picture in the app. We do not transfer it to anyone except as needed to run the service, and never for advertising. Our use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Cookies and this website

The app sets only the cookies needed to keep you signed in. There are no advertising or tracking cookies. This website sets no cookies; Cloudflare counts visits without cookies, fonts load from Google Fonts, and demo videos load from YouTube only when you press play.

How long we keep it

We keep your account, conversations, and files until you ask us to delete them. Email hello@next-role.dev from the address you sign in with and we will delete your account, conversations, files, and saved preferences within 30 days. Copies in server backups expire as the backups rotate. Usage records may be kept in a form that no longer identifies you.

Your choices and rights

You can ask for a copy of your data, a correction, or deletion at any time by email. Depending on where you live, you may have further rights under local law, including the right to complain to your data protection authority. You can also remove NextRole's access to your Google account at myaccount.google.com/connections.

Security

All traffic is encrypted. Your files sit in private storage that is never publicly reachable, and each account can see only its own conversations and files. No system is perfectly secure, so please don't upload anything you would not put on a CV.

Children

NextRole is not intended for anyone under 16, and we do not knowingly collect their data.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update the date above and, for significant changes, tell signed-in users in the app.
